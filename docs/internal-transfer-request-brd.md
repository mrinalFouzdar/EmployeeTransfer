# One-Point Employee Portal — Internal Transfer Request (Client BRD)

## 1. Background
The organisation has a One-Point Employee Portal through which employees can access HR,
payroll, IT, learning, facilities and other employee services.

Currently, an employee requesting an internal transfer needs to interact with multiple teams
and systems. For example:

1. Employee discusses the transfer with the manager.
2. Manager confirms the transfer.
3. HR validates eligibility.
4. Employee's organisational information is updated.
5. Payroll may need to be updated.
6. IT may need to provision or remove access.
7. Facilities may need to arrange the employee's new location.
8. Employee receives confirmation.

The organisation wants to provide a single digital journey through the One-Point Employee
Portal.

## 3. Business Requirement
An employee should be able to initiate an Internal Transfer Request from the One-Point
Employee Portal.

The employee should be able to:
- Select the proposed new department/business unit.
- Select the proposed new location.
- Select the proposed role/job position.
- Provide an effective date.
- Provide an optional reason for the transfer.
- Submit the request.
- View the current status of the request.
- View actions that are pending with other stakeholders.

The organisation wants the portal to orchestrate the downstream activities and provide the
employee with a single view of progress.
